Common questions

What is the minimum amount to start?

Most funds accept a SIP from ₹500 or ₹1,000 a month, and a one-time investment from ₹1,000 to ₹5,000.

Is my money safe with you?

Your money goes directly from your bank to the mutual fund company. Units are held in your name, and we never hold your funds.

What documents do I need?

PAN, Aadhaar linked to your mobile number, and a bank account. KYC is fully online through DigiLocker.

Can I withdraw anytime?

Most open-ended funds can be redeemed on any business day, with money in your bank in 1–3 working days. ELSS funds have a 3-year lock-in.
